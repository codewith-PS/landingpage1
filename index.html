About RG Pleiaddes

RG Pleiaddes is an elite residential development nested in the prime corridor of Sector 1, Noida Extension. Meticulously masterplanned across 8 stunning acres, it establishes a perfect balance of contemporary luxury architecture and functional green spaces. Highlighting an iconic presence, it incorporates 6 majestic towers soaring up to 29 floors.

Emphasizing a rejuvenating lifestyle, over 70% of the land is dedicated to landscape gardens, water features, and jogging tracks. Offering utmost exclusivity and privacy, each tower features private lift lobbies with high-speed elevators. The project features a grand 50,000 sq.ft. clubhouse featuring custom amenities carefully integrated to elevate your daily living experience.
